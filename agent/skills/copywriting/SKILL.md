# Copywriting Skill
Based on MarketingExamples.com & Corey Haines' top principles.

## Core Rules (The "Kernel")

### 1. Clarity > Cleverness (清晰优于聪明)
- **Rule**: If you have to read it twice to understand, rewrite it.
- **Bad**: "Revolutionizing the way you interact with digital assets."
- **Good**: "Save files in one click."

### 2. Specificity (具体化)
- **Rule**: Use numbers, concrete nouns, and physical actions.
- **Bad**: "Save time."
- **Good**: "Save 2 hours per week."

### 3. "I" & "You" (对话感)
- **Rule**: Write as if talking to one person across the table. Use "I" for experience, "You" for benefit.

### 4. The Ban List (禁用词)
- 中文禁用词(综上所述/本质上/颠覆性/革命性/在当今时代等)统一以 `agent/rules/persona.md`「词汇黑名单」为准,不在此重复。
- 英文同理避免:Revolutionize、Game-changing、Unlock(非实体)、In today's digital landscape、In conclusion。

## Headline Formulas (For Step 4)

**Type A: The "How-To" Without Pain**
- "How to [Benefit] without [Pain]."
- Example: "How to get 10k followers without dancing on TikTok."

**Type B: The Counter-Intuitive Truth**
- "[Common Belief] is wrong. Here is why."
- Example: "Posting every day is killing your reach. Here is why."

**Type C: The Experiment Result**
- "I tried [Action] for [Time]. Here is what happened."
- Example: "I tried DeepSeek V2 for 7 days. It scared me."

**Type D: The Timeframe Promise**
- "[Benefit] in [Timeframe]."
- Example: "Master AI Agents in 30 minutes."

## Instruction for Agent
When using this skill to generate headlines:
1. Generate 10 variations using different formulas above.
2. Force yourself to use "Risk/Anxiety" or "Information Gap" in at least 5 of them.
3. Keep length under 20 Chinese characters.
